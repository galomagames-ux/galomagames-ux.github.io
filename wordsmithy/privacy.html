Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026  ·  Last updated: October 28, 2026

About this policy

WordSmithy - Word Hunt is a word game made by Bernardo, an independent developer (“I,” “me”). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, and the choices you have.

WordSmithy - Word Hunt has no accounts of its own. The only ads are optional reward videos you choose to watch in the shop, provided by Google AdMob. I don’t receive your game data except what global play posts to CloudKit (see Global play). Features that connect you with other players use Apple’s Game Center, and purchases use Apple’s App Store.

Information the app keeps on your device

Your scores, daily results, streaks, coins, power-ups, themes, settings and chosen app icon are stored on your device. Deleting the app deletes them.

  • I do not collect your name, email address, contacts or photos. The ad provider estimates your general location from your IP address; see Advertising below.
  • There is nothing to sign in to in the app itself.

Game Center

Leaderboards, friend challenges, live matches and brackets use Apple Game Center. If you are signed in to Game Center, Apple shares your Game Center name and avatar with the players you play against, and your daily scores appear on the leaderboard. In friend matches the app sends the words you found and your scores to your opponent through Game Center. This data is handled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy; I don't receive it. You can use the rest of the app without Game Center.

Purchases

Coin packs are bought through the App Store. Apple handles payment, and I never see your payment details. The app receives only confirmation that a purchase completed.

Advertising

The shop lets you watch a short video ad to earn coins or a power-up. Ads only play when you choose to watch one; the app shows no other ads.

Ads are provided by Google AdMob. When an ad loads, Google may collect:

  • Your device’s advertising identifier, only if you allow tracking
  • Your IP address, used to estimate your general location
  • Information about how you interact with ads
  • Crash and performance data from the ad software

Google uses this to show ads, measure them and prevent fraud. I don’t receive this data. Learn more in Google’s privacy policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Your choices: The app asks whether it may track you before showing personalized ads. If you choose “Ask App Not to Track,” your advertising identifier isn’t shared and you’ll see non-personalized ads. You can change this anytime in Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. Where the law requires it (such as in Europe and the UK), adults also see a consent form from Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) before any ads load, and can reopen it any time from Me > Privacy choices. You never have to watch an ad to play the game.

Age: the app asks Apple for your age range (under 13, 13 to 17, or 18 and over) through Apple’s Declared Age Range feature. It never asks for your birthdate, and Apple decides what to share. I use it only to choose ad settings and never save it. If you are under 13, or the app can’t tell your age, ads are child-directed and non-personalized and you won’t see the tracking prompt. If you are 13 to 17, ads are non-personalized. Only adults who allow tracking get personalized ads.

Global play

Global play lets you compete in a shared round with other players around the world. When a round ends, the app posts one record to Apple’s CloudKit public database (in this app’s own CloudKit container) containing:

  • Your Game Center player ID (an identifier Apple gives your Game Center account for this game, not your Apple ID or email)
  • Your Game Center display name (shortened to 20 characters)
  • Your chosen in-game avatar
  • Your score and the round number
  • The words you found, stored as the board positions you traced, not as text

This is used only to build the round’s rankings and to check that scores are real. You must be signed in to iCloud and Game Center to post. Anyone playing global play can see names, avatars and scores on the rankings. Players under 13 see other players as “Player 1”, “Player 2” and so on.

How long it’s kept: global play records are kept for 90 days and then deleted. The app removes your own records older than 90 days when you open global play, and I also clear any older records from the database myself. Until then they are stored by Apple, and I can see them in my CloudKit dashboard. I do not use them for advertising, analytics or anything beyond the rankings, and I do not sell or share them. To have your records removed sooner, email me at the address below and include your Game Center name.

To keep every player on the same clock, the app also asks this website for the time (a plain request, like opening a web page, with no personal information).

Sharing

When you tap Share, the app creates a short message and a picture (your score, your name if you are signed in to Game Center, and similar details) and gives them to the iOS share sheet. Nothing is sent anywhere unless you choose an app and send it. Shared daily results contain numbers only, never the words you found.

Children's privacy

WordSmithy - Word Hunt is a general-audience game. I do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children. Ads in the app are optional and are not targeted at children.
