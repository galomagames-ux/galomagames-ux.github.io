About

WordSmithy

A fast word game: trace words through a grid of letters before time runs out.

What's inside

  • 4×4, 5×5 and 6×6 boards, with special two-letter tiles worth bonus points
  • A daily board that's the same for everyone, with streaks and rewards
  • Play Lex the robot at five difficulty levels, or take on Grimbly the goblin when he shows up
  • Challenge friends (on your own time or live) and run friend brackets through Game Center
  • Power-ups, board themes and three app icons
