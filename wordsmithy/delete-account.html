Delete your WordSmithy account

How to delete your online WordSmithy - Word Hunt account and data on Android (made by Galoma Games).

Steps

  1. Open WordSmithy and tap Me.
  2. Tap Account.
  3. Tap Delete my online account, then tap Delete to confirm.

This works whether or not you signed in with Google. If the app asks you to sign in with Google again first, do that and tap Delete once more.

Can’t open the app? Email galomagames@gmail.com from any address and include your friend code (shown under Play > Friends > Add friend) or the Google account email you signed in with. I will delete the account and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your online profile: generated name, avatar and friend code
  • Your friends list and friend connections
  • Your challenge records and two-player matches
  • Your global and daily scores, and the words you traced for them
  • Your notification token, if you turned notifications on
  • Your sign-in: the anonymous player ID and, if you linked it, the Google sign-in (including the email address Google gave Firebase Authentication)

What is kept, and for how long

  • Brackets with three or more players are shared with the other players, so they are not removed. You are marked as having quit, and the record is deleted automatically 30 days after its last move. It shows only your anonymous player ID and generated name.
  • Data on your phone (coins, streaks, settings, nicknames you gave friends) stays on your phone. Uninstalling the app removes it.
  • Other players’ phones may still show a result or a nickname they saved for you. That is stored only on their phone.
  • If you never delete your account, online data is removed automatically: scores and matches after 30 days, profiles and friend connections after 90 days without opening the app.
